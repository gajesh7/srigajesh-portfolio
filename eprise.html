01 The Vision

Making everyday business management simpler.

ePrise was designed to bring essential accounting and business operations into one connected platform.

The product supports businesses with everyday tasks such as invoicing, GST compliance, inventory management, order booking, and financial operations.

The goal was to reduce complexity across business workflows and make important information easier to access, understand, and act on.

04 / The Challenge

Simplifying complex business workflows without losing important information.

01

Managing Complexity

Accounting and business software often contains large amounts of information and multiple interconnected workflows. The challenge was to simplify the interface without hiding information users need to make decisions.

02

Supporting Different Business Needs

ePrise serves SMEs and MSMEs with different operational requirements. The experience needed to remain flexible while maintaining a consistent design language across the product.

03

Making Compliance Understandable

GST, invoices, and related business processes can be difficult for non-technical users. The interface needed to present these workflows in a clear and approachable way.

04

Designing for Frequent Use

Accounting and inventory tools are used repeatedly throughout the working day. The design needed to prioritize speed, clarity, consistency, and easy access to commonly used actions.

05 UX & Design Approach

Turning complex workflows into clear, repeatable journeys.

I worked across user flows, information architecture, wireframes, and interface design to simplify the product experience. The focus was on understanding how users move between accounting, invoicing, inventory, orders, and compliance workflows.

01 / CREATE

Create Customers, Products, Invoices & Orders

Designed for high-speed input with streamlined form fields, keyboard shortcuts, and intelligent autocomplete for frequent accounts and catalog items.

02 / MANAGE

Manage Transactions, Inventory, Payments & Records

Central operational layer keeping cash flow, general ledger entries, supplier balances, and warehouse stock continuously synchronized.

03 / TRACK

Monitor Orders, Stock, Invoices & Financial Activity

Real-time tracking dashboards highlighting inventory buffer levels, overdue accounts, payment receipts, and dispatch states.

04 / COMPLY

Manage GST-Related Documents & Compliance Workflows

One-click IRN generation, automated E-Way bills, and live reconciliation between purchase registers and GSTR-2B portal filings.

05 / REVIEW

Access Reports & Business Information for Decisions

Executive summaries, P&L trajectories, tax liability previews, and product margin analyses structured for clarity and fast decision-making.

08 Design Learnings

Designing for complexity without making the product feel complex.

Working on ePrise highlighted the importance of balancing information density with usability.

Business software needs to provide users with a lot of information, but that information still needs to be structured in a way that supports quick decisions.

The project strengthened my approach to designing:
  • Complex workflows
  • Data-heavy interfaces
  • Business dashboards
  • Form-heavy experiences
  • Reusable design patterns
  • Multi-step processes
