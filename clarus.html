02 Capabilities

Engineered for every stage of production.

01 — SMT & THROUGH-HOLE

High-Speed SMT & THT

Fast, accurate surface-mount and through-hole technology for complex PCB assemblies. Supports ultra-fine pitch components, dual-sided reflow, and automated wave soldering with exceptional yield rates.

01005 to 45mm BGAs Dual-Lane SMT Lines Nitrogen Reflow Selective Soldering
02 — CUSTOM TOOLING

Precision Plastic Moulding

Custom tooling and high-tonnage electric injection moulding for complete hardware enclosures, custom connectors, and structural plastic parts. Engineered for tight tolerances and demanding thermal environments.

  • High-precision electric injection presses (50T to 450T)
  • Engineering resins: ABS, PC, Nylon, PBT, and ESD-safe blends
  • In-house toolmaking, rapid cavity inserts, and ultrasonic assembly
03 — QUALITY ASSURANCE

Zero-Defect Validation

Multi-stage quality control, automated optical inspection, and comprehensive electrical testing at every production checkpoint ensure zero-defect shipment integrity.

  • 3D Solder Paste Inspection (SPI) & Automated Optical Inspection (AOI)
  • Real-time X-ray inspection for hidden BGA/QFN solder voids
  • In-circuit testing (ICT), functional testing (FCT), and burn-in screening

04 Design Approach

Turning technical expertise into a clear digital experience.

Hardware manufacturing websites often suffer from extreme information density, outdated imagery, or vague marketing speak. Our strategy was to respect the technical depth that hardware engineers need, while presenting capabilities with the visual elegance and scannability of a modern digital product.

01 — ARCHITECTURE HIERARCHY

Layered Technical Content

Technical specifications are structured into progressive layers: high-level capacity metrics upfront, followed by detailed equipment tolerances (01005 passives, BGA ball pitch, press tonnage) for procurement specialists.

02 — AESTHETIC IDENTITY

Industrial Modern Tone

Deep slate charcoal, warm studio off-white, and precision industrial orange create a technical yet inviting aesthetic that reflects cleanroom manufacturing environments and high-precision machinery.

03 — CONVERSION INBOUND RFQS

Streamlined Quote Flow

Interactive project scope selectors and BOM (Bill of Materials) upload guidance reduce friction, enabling engineering teams to request accurate quotations without lengthy back-and-forth discovery calls.

08 Project Reflection

Designing for a technical audience.

Designing for hardware and electronics manufacturing requires a deep respect for precision and authenticity. Hardware founders and procurement engineers are naturally skeptical of flashy visuals that lack substance.

The most rewarding challenge was turning complex manufacturing specifications—such as component size ranges, SMT lines, reflow oven zones, and testing protocols—into clear, scannable, and credible interfaces without simplifying away the technical truth.

By structuring technical details into progressive disclosure layers, the website allows quick decision-making for executive buyers while providing the deep technical verification demanded by hardware engineers.
