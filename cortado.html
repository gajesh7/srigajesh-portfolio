01 The Vision

Transforming the morning coffee routine into a flawless digital ritual.

Cortado is a café ordering experience designed to connect customers with their favorite café through four distinct fulfillment channels: delivery, dine-in, takeaway, and curbside car pickup.

Whether grabbing an iced Spanish latte on the way to work or settling in for an afternoon cold brew, Cortado provides a frictionless, appetizing mobile journey paired with an integrated loyalty system and digital wallet.

Frictionless Curbside Arrival: The "I'm Outside" Innovation

Morning drive-thru queues are often congested and chaotic. We engineered an intuitive 4-stage tracking status: Preparing → Ready → Completed → I'm Outside.

When pulling up to the café parking bay, the customer taps the prominent terracotta "I'm Outside" button. The barista counter receives an instantaneous audio-visual ping with the vehicle and order number, ensuring the espresso is handed over at peak crema without leaving the driver's seat.

03 / The Challenge

Balancing complex café logistics with an effortless guest experience.

01

Four Fulfillment Channels Without Clutter

Café patrons have drastically differing needs: some want express car pickup while commuting, others want table-side dine-in QR ordering or home delivery. The interface had to unify all four without confusing branch inventories or operating hours.

02

Ending Parking Lot Queue Friction

Traditional café drive-thrus suffer from congestion and delays. We needed a frictionless protocol for drivers to announce their arrival without having to place phone calls or honk outside the café.

03

Frictionless Loyalty & Instant Wallet Payments

Physical punch cards get lost, and standard mobile checkout with credit card verification takes too long during morning rush hours. Cortado needed a frictionless digital wallet with automatic reward points accumulation.

04 UX & Design Architecture

Engineering a seamless journey from discovery to the first sip.

We mapped out every micro-touchpoint in the customer journey across Miro and Figma. From fast branch selection to custom drink modifications (oat milk, sweetness levels, single-origin roasts), every interaction was stripped of superfluous steps.

Key insights into the Cortado design process and execution.

Cortado is a bespoke mobile café app designed to make ordering, pickup, delivery, rewards, membership, wallet, and customer interactions effortless. It caters to multi-branch operations across Bahrain, connecting specialty coffee connoisseurs directly with barista stations.

I served as the Lead UI/UX Designer, overseeing the entire digital product lifecycle from initial product discovery and stakeholder interviews to information architecture, wireframing, high-fidelity UI design, component systems, and engineering handover.

I eliminated morning peak-hour bottlenecks by unifying four fulfillment methods (Delivery, Dine-in, Takeaway, and Curbside Car-Pickup) into a cohesive flow, creating the "I'm Outside" arrival trigger to remove drive-thru congestion, and introducing an integrated digital wallet that speeds up checkout to under 3 seconds.

The complete design and iteration phase spanned 2 months from conceptual kickoff to developer design tokens and high-fidelity prototype verification.

I designed the full end-to-end mobile experience: Onboarding, Branch Selection & Filters, Home Dashboard, Menu Catalog, Drink Customizer Modal, Cart & Express Checkout, Curbside Tracking & Arrival Dispatch, Membership Tiers, Rewards Voucher Redemption, Digital Wallet, and User Profile.
